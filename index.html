About

An upscale pub built around the tide.

Coad n Cove pairs a considered drinks list with fresh, seasonal plates in a room made for lingering. Whether it's a quiet pint after work or a table for a celebration, we hold a spot for you when you book ahead.

Walk-ins are welcome as space allows, but for evenings and weekends we'd recommend reserving your table below.
